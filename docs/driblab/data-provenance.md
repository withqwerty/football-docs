## Driblab data sources

**Category:** first-party (tracking); not publicly documented (other data).

Driblab's tracking data comes from its own optical tracking system, which uses
computer vision on match video. The page does not say which video it uses.
Driblab does not publicly document where its other statistics come from.

Source: [Driblab tracking](https://www.driblab.com/tracking): "Our AI football
optical tracking system follows the position of the 22 players and the ball
every tenth of a second" and "Through computer vision techniques, the system
detects and extracts key game features automatically." Checked 2026-09-24.
