# TransferRoom data provenance

## TransferRoom data sources

**Category:** first-party.

TransferRoom's valuation and transfer-feasibility data comes from activity on
its own transfer marketplace, verified by its network of clubs.

Source: [TransferRoom API](https://blog.transferroom.com/transferroom-api-smarter-team-building-powered-by-data):
"a growing percentage of global transfers now happen on TransferRoom and that
activity provides us with a unique view into the underlying valuation data and
transfer feasibility" and "This data is updated in real time, verified by
TransferRoom's global network". Checked 2026-09-24.
