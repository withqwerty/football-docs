# Impect data provenance

## Impect data sources

**Category:** first-party.

Impect, now part of Catapult, collects all of its data in-house, with its own
collection team in the Philippines. Its metrics, such as packing, are computed
from that data.

The other docs in this folder are grounded in Impect's public open-data
repository, github.com/ImpectAPI/open-data.

Source: [Catapult IMPECT](https://www.catapult.com/blog/impect-data-from-football-for-football):
"We collect all data internally with our collection team based in The
Philippines. This allows us to control how we collect our data & ensure the
highest possible quality is generated." Checked 2026-09-24.
